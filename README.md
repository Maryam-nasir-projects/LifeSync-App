# LifeSync — Student Productivity and Digital Well-being App

LifeSync is a student-focused productivity and digital well-being app designed to help university students manage their study routines, screen time, and daily habits.

The project was developed as part of the Design Thinking course at Al-Ghazali University.

## Live App

https://maryam-nasir-projects.github.io/LifeSync-App/

## GitHub Repository

https://github.com/Maryam-nasir-projects/LifeSync-App

## Features

- **Dashboard:** View your productivity and progress.
- **Study Scheduler:** Organize study tasks and schedules.
- **Focus Mode:** Use an in-app Pomodoro-style focus session.
- **Screen-Time Tracker:** Record and review screen-time habits.
- **Mood Journal:** Reflect on your mood.
- **Sleep Sounds:** Access sounds for a relaxing environment.
- **Digital Balance Challenges:** Complete challenges for healthier digital habits.
- **Reality Lens:** Understand social comparison on social media.
- **Cyber Safety Guide:** Learn about online safety.
- **Safety & Shield:** Access safety guidance and trusted-contact support.
- **Compass and Rewards:** Track progress and achievements.
- **Dark Mode:** Switch the app's appearance.
- **PWA Support:** Install the app through a compatible browser.

## How to Use

1. Open the live app link.
2. Explore the dashboard and available features.
3. Use the Study Scheduler to organize your tasks.
4. Try Focus Mode for study sessions.
5. Track your habits and explore digital balance challenges.
6. On a compatible browser, use the **Install App** option to install LifeSync.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of LifeSync is to help students build better study routines and become more aware of their digital habits. It combines productivity tools with digital well-being guidance in one application.

## Limitations

LifeSync is a student project, not a medical or mental-health service. Screen-time and mood information may require manual entry. Focus Mode works within the app and does not block other apps on the device.
